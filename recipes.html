Culinary Tips / 烹饪指南

The Art of Hotpot

Master the perfect shabu-shabu experience at home. From mixing the ultimate dipping sauce to prepping our premium cuts.

Classic Sesame Dip
经典麻酱蘸料
⏱ 2 Mins
  • 3 tbsp JoyShare Sesame Paste
  • 1 tbsp Light Soy Sauce
  • 1 tsp Fermented Bean Curd (optional)
  • Top with fresh cilantro and scallions
Perfect Mala Base
正宗麻辣汤底
⏱ 5 Mins
  • Add 1 pack Little Sheep Mala to 1.5L water
  • Add 4-5 slices of fresh ginger
  • Throw in a handful of dried red chilies
  • Boil for 5 mins before adding meats
The 15-Second Rule
涮肉金律
Pro Tip

For our **Beef Short Plate** and **Lamb Shoulder**, do not overcook! Dip the roll into boiling soup for exactly 15-20 seconds until it just turns from pink to brown. This preserves the "melt-in-your-mouth" texture.
