Culinary Inspiration / 烹饪灵感

Home Cooking Guides

Turn our premium cuts into delicious daily meals. / 用我们的优质肉类开启美味日常。

Pressure Cooker / 压力锅

Tomato & Beef "Finger" Stew

西红柿炖牛腩块

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Sear Finger Meat in the Instant Pot until browned.
    在压力锅中将牛指肉煎至金黄。
  2. Add diced tomatoes, ginger slices, and a splash of soy sauce.
    加入番茄块、姜片和生抽。
  3. Pressure cook for 35 mins. The beef becomes melt-in-your-mouth tender.
    高压烹饪35分钟，让牛肉入口即化。
Slow Cook / 慢炖

Clear Daikon & Lamb Stew

白萝卜炖羊肉

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Parboil Lamb Leg Cubes to remove impurities, then rinse.
    将羊腿肉块焯水去浮沫，洗净。
  2. Stew with Daikon, ginger, and scallions for 1.5 hours.
    与白萝卜、姜片和葱段中小火慢炖1.5小时。
  3. Season with salt and white pepper for a clean, warming broth.
    加入盐和白胡椒粉，汤头清甜暖胃。
Side Dish / 凉拌小菜

Sesame Glass Noodle Salad

凉拌红薯粉

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Boil Sweet Potato Noodles for 8 mins until chewy.
    将红薯粉丝煮8分钟至口感筋道。
  2. Toss with spinach, carrots, soy sauce, and Sesame Oil.
    拌入菠菜、胡萝卜丝、生抽和香油。
  3. Top with toasted sesame seeds for a savory banchan style dish.
    撒上芝麻，制作成美味的韩式凉拌菜。
Spicy Pot / 辣锅

Spicy Soft Tofu Stew with Beef

牛肉嫩豆腐锅

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Sauté garlic and chili flakes, add broth and Soft Tofu.
    煸香蒜末和辣椒面，加入汤底和嫩豆腐。
  2. Drop in Beef Short Plate Rolls; they cook instantly.
    放入牛五花卷，肉片会迅速煮熟。
  3. Finish with a raw egg and green onions while bubbling.
    趁热打入生鸡蛋并撒上葱花。
High Heat / 爆炒

"Mala" Sliced Lamb Stir-Fry

麻辣爆炒羊肉

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Heat oil until smoking; add Lamb Shoulder Rolls.
    大火热油，倒入羊肉卷快速翻炒。
  2. Add dried chilies and Szechuan peppercorns for a numbing kick.
    加入干辣椒和花椒，炒出麻辣香味。
  3. Quick sear for 2 mins to keep the lamb tender and smoky.
    快速爆炒2分钟，保持羊肉鲜嫩出镬气。
Fusion / 慢炖

Red Wine Slow-Braised Oxtail

红酒烩牛尾

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Dredge Ox Tail in flour and sear all sides.
    牛尾裹少许面粉，各面煎至焦黄。
  2. Slow simmer with red wine, carrots, and onions for 3 hours.
    加入红酒、胡萝卜和洋葱，小火慢炖3小时。
  3. The sauce will thicken into a rich gravy.
    汤汁会浓缩成醇厚的肉汁。
5-Min Meal / 快捷午餐

Beef Short Plate "Gyudon" Bowl

日式肥牛饭

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Simmer sliced onions in Soy Sauce, sugar, and dashi.
    用生抽、糖和高汤煮软洋葱丝。
  2. Add Beef Short Plate Rolls until just cooked.
    加入牛五花卷煮至变色。
  3. Pour over hot rice for an instant savory meal.
    淋在热米饭上，简单美味。
Steamed / 叠叠锅

Napa & Beef "Thousand Layer" Pot

白菜肥牛叠叠锅

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Layer Napa leaves with Beef Chuck Rolls in a pot.
    在锅中交替叠放白菜叶和牛眉头卷。
  2. Steam with a dash of dashi or water for 10 mins.
    加入少许高汤或水，盖盖焖煮10分钟。
  3. The cabbage juice creates a sweet, natural broth.
    白菜煮出的汁水会形成天然清甜的汤底。
High Heat / 葱爆

Stir-Fried Mutton with Scallion

葱爆有骨羊

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Sear Mutton Leg Cubes over very high heat.
    大火快速煎熟有骨老羊腿块。
  2. Add plenty of sliced Green Onions and leeks.
    加入大量的葱段或大葱。
  3. Season with soy sauce and vinegar for a classic Beijing style fry.
    加入生抽和陈醋，做出经典的京味葱爆羊肉。
Textured Stir-Fry / 腐竹小炒

Tofu Skin & Meat Roll Stir-Fry

腐竹肥牛小炒

Directions / 步骤:
  1. Soak Tofu Skin until soft, then stir-fry with peppers.
    将腐竹泡软，与青椒一同翻炒。
  2. Add Beef Short Plate Rolls at the end to keep them tender.
    最后加入牛五花卷，保持肉质鲜嫩。
  3. The tofu skin absorbs all the beef juices.
    让腐竹充分吸收牛肉的油脂和香气。
